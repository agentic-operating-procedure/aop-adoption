# Changelog

All notable changes to this repository are documented here. Format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/). Versioning is semantic: the major version changes when a playbook stage or a maturity stage changes in meaning, the minor version when substantive adoption content is added, and the patch version for corrections that do not change meaning.

## [Unreleased]

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-05

### Added (Wave H5: CR-019)

- The operating modes and promotion gates: five modes per action class (observe-only, recommend, approval-gated, bounded, expanded) with entry, promotion, demotion and stop criteria; the conformance-versus-authorisation distinction; the mapping onto the maturity model. Authority is never widened by a mode; promotion consumes the evaluation tier's protocol (CR-031).


## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-04

### Adoption foundations

- Published the six-stage procedural engineering playbook: decomposition, operational grounding, autonomy disposition, AOP specification, harness engineering, verification and assurance, each with a gate and evidence obligations.
- Published the maturity model: human-operated, assisted, agentic, autonomous, with the honest-stage rule and regression reporting.
- Published the three adoption scenarios: pilot, brownfield, regulated and high-risk, with entry conditions and traps.
- Published the organisational change guidance: roles, decision rights, and the shift from corridor execution to exception ownership.
- Published the adopter checklists: one per stage, steward-free.
