# AOP Adoption

`aop-adoption` is the single authority for how an organisation moves from Standard Operating Procedures to Agentic Operating Procedures: the six-stage procedural engineering playbook, the maturity model, the three adoption scenarios, and the organisational change guidance.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Authority | The migration: playbook, maturity model, scenarios, change guidance |
| Tier | Adoption |
| Landing wave | 6 |
| Version | 0.1.0 |
| Status | Stable |
| Licence | MIT |

## What this repository owns

- The six-stage procedural engineering playbook: procedural decomposition, operational grounding, autonomy disposition, AOP specification, harness engineering, verification and assurance.
- The maturity model from human-operated through assisted to agentic to autonomous operations.
- The three adoption scenarios: pilot, brownfield, regulated and high-risk.
- The organisational change guidance: roles, decision rights, and the shift from corridor execution to exception ownership.
- The adopter-facing checklists, usable without the programme stewards in the room.

## What this repository does not own

- The tooling that implements the playbook steps. That is `aop-toolkit`.
- The complete worked demonstrations the playbook points at. That is `aop-examples`.

## Who this is for

A team that has an SOP and a desire to operate it with an agent. The playbook assumes no programme steward present; every stage states what to do, what evidence to keep, and what gate must pass before the next stage. The worked demonstration of the whole path is the 5G anomaly management exemplar in `aop-examples`.
